2 Portal & Role Breakdown

This section explains the critical difference between the two specialized portals: Medico and Leadership. If your online pharmacy was a physical brick-and-mortar store, the Medico acts as the Head Pharmacist managing the counter and inventory, while the Leadership acts as the Store Manager or Owner working in the back office handling finances, marketing, and strategy.

The "Medico" Portal

Target Audience: Pharmacists, Inventory Managers, & Dispensary Staff

The Medico's primary responsibility is ensuring clinical accuracy and inventory fulfillment. They do not worry about profit margins or marketing campaigns; their sole focus is making sure that the correct medicines are in stock, properly categorized, safely dispensed, and legally verified. They are the medical gatekeepers of your platform.

Dashboard

Provides a daily operational checklist. Medicos can immediately see how many orders are pending packaging and dispatch, and how many new prescriptions have been uploaded by patients that require urgent medical review.

Orders

The fulfillment center. Medicos use this tab to physically process sales. When a customer buys something, the Medico prints the packing slip, verifies the physical stock, boxes the items, and marks the order as "Shipped".

Medicines

The master inventory list. Medicos have full control over the catalog. They can add new drugs, update MRP and discount prices, update physical stock levels, input HSN codes for GST, and define active compositions and side effects.

Generate AI

A productivity tool that helps Medicos quickly write professional, legally-compliant medical descriptions for new catalog items without spending hours typing them manually.

Prescriptions (Core Clinical Duty)

This is their most critical clinical function. For medicines that require a doctor's note (Rx), patients will upload images of their prescriptions. The Medico, as a licensed professional, reviews these images to ensure they are valid, within date, and authentic before approving the order to be shipped.

The "Leadership" Portal

Target Audience: Business Owners, Financial Managers, & Customer Experience Directors

The Leadership team focuses exclusively on business growth, tracking revenue, auditing finances, and ensuring the customer experience is absolutely flawless. They do not handle the day-to-day packing of pills; they handle the money, the people, and the high-level strategy.

Dashboard

Shows high-level business intelligence metrics. Leadership can track daily/monthly revenue, user growth rates, average order values, and overall profit margins at a glance.

Orders & Transactions

While Medicos use "Orders" to pack boxes, Leadership uses this section to audit finances. They track every payment gateway transaction, resolve failed payments, process customer refunds, and ensure the bank deposits match the system records.

Reward Points

A powerful marketing tool. Leadership dictates how the loyalty program works—setting rules for how many points customers earn per purchase, and establishing the conversion rate for when customers redeem points for discounts to drive repeat business.

Doctors

Allows Leadership to manage B2B relationships and partnerships with medical professionals who operate on the platform, track their consultation volume, and manage their payouts.

Support Tickets & Bot Simulator

Oversees customer satisfaction. Leadership can review escalated customer complaints or disputes. Additionally, they use the Bot Simulator to train and test the automated Chatbot to ensure it gives polite, accurate answers to customers automatically.

3 Master Admin Features Glossary

The Master Admin portal is the highest level of access. It combines every single feature of the Medico and Leadership portals, plus exclusive system configuration settings. Below is a deep dive into exactly what every single tab accomplishes.

📊 Dashboard

What it is: The command center of your store.
Detailed Explanation: Think of this as the "front page" of your business. It gives you a quick snapshot of how your store is doing today—showing your total sales revenue, how many new orders came in, new user registrations, and quick alerts on stock shortages or pending prescriptions that need your immediate attention.

👥 Users & 🛡️ Roles

What it is: Customer and Staff management.
Detailed Explanation: The Users tab is your customer address book. You can view everyone who has created an account, see their purchase history, reset their passwords, or block malicious accounts. The Roles tab allows you to create employee accounts (like a Support Agent) and strictly control what they are allowed to see and do, ensuring they can't access sensitive financial data.

📦 Orders & 💳 Transactions

What it is: The heart of your operations and financial history.
Detailed Explanation: The Orders section is where you process physical sales. You can update the status of a purchase from "Processing" to "Packed" to "Shipped", generate PDF tax invoices, and print shipping labels. The Transactions tab acts as your digital cash register tape—it tracks the exact status of every payment gateway transaction, including successful payments, failed cards, and any refunds you've issued.

💊 Medicines & 🤖 Generate AI

What it is: Your product inventory and AI assistant.
Detailed Explanation: The Medicines tab is where you add the actual products you are selling. You can upload images, set the MRP, apply discount prices, update physical stock levels, and assign HSN codes for accurate GST billing. The Generate AI tool is a smart assistant that helps you write these product descriptions automatically in seconds, saving you hours of manual typing.

🗂️ Categories, 🏢 Brands, & 🚚 Suppliers

What it is: Product organization and vendor management.
Detailed Explanation: Categories act as the "aisles" of your digital store (e.g., "Vitamins", "Pain Relief") so customers can browse easily. Brands is a list of the pharmaceutical companies whose products you sell (like Cipla, Sun Pharma). Suppliers is a private address book of the wholesale distributors who supply you with stock, making it easy to know who to call when you need to reorder.

📝 Prescriptions

What it is: Clinical Rx verification.
Detailed Explanation: For medicines that legally require a doctor's note, customers will upload a photo of their prescription during checkout. This tab allows your pharmacist to review the uploaded image, verify the doctor's signature and date, and either approve or reject the order based on clinical validity.

🩺 Doctors & 📅 Appointments

What it is: Doctor directory and Consultation bookings.
Detailed Explanation: A database of doctors partnered with your platform. If your platform offers online telemedicine consultations, this is the calendar where you track and manage all upcoming and past patient bookings, as well as manage the doctors' profiles and schedules.

📰 Blogs, ❓ FAQs, & 🔍 SEO Rules

What it is: Content marketing and Search Engine Optimization.
Detailed Explanation: Blogs allow you to write health articles to attract visitors from Google. FAQs provide a self-help section so customers can find answers to common questions (like "How long does delivery take?") without calling support. SEO Rules give you technical control over meta titles and keywords to ensure your store ranks high on Google search results.

🎧 Support Tickets & 🗑️ Data Deletion

What it is: Customer service desk and Privacy compliance.
Detailed Explanation: When a customer has a complaint or a question about their order, it creates a "ticket" here. You and your staff can reply to them directly from this panel until the issue is resolved. Data Deletion allows you to safely process requests from users who want their accounts and personal data erased, keeping you compliant with privacy laws.

🎟️ Coupons & 🖼️ Banners

What it is: Promotions and Homepage advertisements.
Detailed Explanation: Coupons allow you to create special discount codes (like "DIWALI20" for 20% off) and set rules like minimum order amounts or expiry dates. Banners allow you to easily swap out the big, eye-catching promotional images on the front page of your app or website to advertise sales or popular products.

💬 Bot Simulator

What it is: Chatbot testing.
Detailed Explanation: A safe testing ground for your automated AI customer service bot. You can chat with it here to simulate customer interactions, ensuring it gives polite, accurate answers about your store policies before you deploy it to interact with real customers.

🎁 Reward Points & 👤 Admin Profile

What it is: Loyalty program and Personal settings.
Detailed Explanation: Reward Points is a system to keep customers coming back by allowing you to configure how points are earned and spent. Admin Profile is simply where you manage your own account details, update your login email, change your password, and set up your security preferences.

4 Customer Portal & User Dashboard

This section breaks down the features available to your registered customers when they log into the DoseBox platform. It also details the specific business logic regarding order cancellations, refunds, and prescription handling.

Customer Dashboard Sections

  • Dashboard: A quick welcome screen showing their recent orders, current wallet balance, and quick links.

  • My Prescriptions: A digital health locker. Users can view all their historically uploaded prescriptions and check whether the Admin has Approved or Rejected them.

  • My Consultations: A history log of all their online telemedicine appointments booked through the platform.

  • Reward Points: Their digital wallet. Users can track how many DoseBox Tokens they have earned, and see a history of when they spent them on previous orders.

  • Manage Addresses: Allows users to save multiple delivery locations (e.g., Home, Office, Parents' House) for faster checkout.

  • Personal Information: Where the user can update their name, contact phone number, and change their password.

My Orders & Invoices

In the My Orders tab, customers can view their complete purchase history. Clicking on an individual order opens the Order Details page.

  • Detailed Breakdown: Users see a full breakdown of their purchase, including exact items ordered, GST/Tax calculations, promo discounts applied, and shipping charges.

  • PDF Invoices: Once an order is processed, customers can instantly download a highly detailed, professional, GST-compliant PDF Tax Invoice for their personal health records or medical insurance claims.

  • Order Tracking: Users can track the live status of their order (e.g., Pending → Processing → Packed → Shipped → Delivered).

Prescription Rules & Cases

The platform dynamically handles prescriptions based on what the user is buying:

  • Non-Rx Orders: If a cart only contains general wellness items (like Vitamins or Shampoo), the user can check out instantly without uploading anything.

  • Rx Orders (Upload Required): If the cart contains a restricted drug, the checkout is strictly paused. The user MUST upload an image of a valid doctor's prescription to proceed.

  • Admin Approval: Once uploaded, the order status becomes "Pending Approval". The Medico team reviews the image. If approved, the order begins processing and is shipped.

  • Admin Rejection: If the prescription is invalid, blurry, or expired, the Medico rejects it. The order is halted, and the user is notified to provide a valid prescription or their order is canceled and refunded.

Cancellations, Refunds & DoseBox Tokens Policy

DoseBox has a very specific, customer-friendly wallet logic to handle canceled orders and compensate users.

1. User-Initiated Cancellations (The 2-Time Limit)

Customers are allowed to cancel an order from their dashboard up to 2 times for any personal reason. As a goodwill gesture, when they use these free cancellations, they are compensated with DoseBox Tokens (Reward Points) added directly to their wallet:

  • If the canceled Order Value is less than ₹500 → The user receives 50 DoseBox Tokens.
  • If the canceled Order Value is ₹500 or more → The user receives 100 DoseBox Tokens.

2. Exceeding the Cancellation Limit

Once a user reaches their strict 2-time cancellation limit, they can no longer freely cancel orders and farm reward tokens. If they need to cancel a 3rd order, they must reach out to Customer Support. For these excess cancellations, they will receive a standard monetary refund as per their original order amount, but they will not receive any bonus DoseBox Tokens.

3. Admin-Initiated Cancellations (Platform Fault)

If the Admin has to cancel an order (for example, due to an item being Out of Stock, a delivery logistics issue, or a rejected prescription), the user is not penalized. Because this is an administrative fault, the user automatically receives a full 100% monetary refund to their original payment method for the exact order amount.

5 Deep Dive: Core Platform Features

A closer look at the advanced logic powering your platform's most powerful capabilities.

How "Upload Prescription" Works

Handling restricted medical drugs legally and safely is a core feature of the platform. Here is the end-to-end flow.

1

Seamless Upload & AI Scanning

Customers can upload a photo of their doctor's note or a written format name. The system's AI automatically scans and reads the image to identify the medicines.

2

Instant Non-Rx Checkout & Alternatives

If the AI identifies the medicine as Non-Rx (over-the-counter), the user can directly add it to their cart and checkout instantly. The system also intelligently recommends alternative medicines by showing salt names and compositions so the user has cheaper or better options.

3

Admin Review (Rx & Unreadable)

If the medicine requires a prescription (Rx), or if the AI is unable to clearly read the handwritten text, the application automatically goes into "Admin Review".

4

Approval & Add to Cart

The Medico reviews the uploaded image, manually adds the correct medicine to the user's application, and approves it. The user then sees "Application Approved" in their prescription section, along with a direct "Add to Cart" button for the exact medicines the admin approved.

AI Customer Service Chatbot

DoseBox includes a 24/7 intelligent AI Chatbot designed to drastically reduce the workload of human support agents by automatically answering customer queries and driving direct sales.

💬

Instant Customer Support

Customers can access the chatbot on the homepage at any time. The bot can instantly answer normal questions about store policies, delivery times, refund statuses, and how to navigate the website.

🛒

Direct Cart Integration

This is the most powerful feature. If a user types a medicine name, the AI directly queries the database. If in stock, it immediately provides a direct "Add to Cart" button right inside the chat window.
